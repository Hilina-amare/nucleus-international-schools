Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about curriculum, admissions, fees, and campus life.

Nucleus teaches the official Cambridge International Pathway from Early Years through Grade 8. It is a globally recognized, rigorous curriculum respected by universities and international schools worldwide, making it completely portable if your family relocates internationally.

We accept children from age 2 (Toddler & Early Years) through Grade 8 (Lower Secondary / Cambridge Checkpoint). Students transition smoothly between our specialized Early Years Abo Campus and our Grade School Vatican Campus.

We currently have two operational campuses: 1. Vatican Grade School Campus: Sarbet Vatican, 50m behind the Vatican Embassy. 2. Abo Preschool Campus: 100m from Mekanisa Abo Square. Our third campus at Totot (behind World Vision) is opening soon.

We maintain total transparency with no hidden capital building funds. Tuition covers textbooks, Cambridge learning materials, and basic laboratory equipment. Optional services include chef-prepared nutrition and door-to-door bus transportation. Call our admissions team on 09 81 99 99 22 for your child's grade breakdown.

Safety is our highest priority. Located inside the quiet embassy enclave behind the Vatican Embassy, our campus features controlled perimeter gating, visitor identity verification, and 24/7 guarded premises.

Both are prestigious curricula. While the IB is heavily project-based, Cambridge offers unmatched academic structure, clear external benchmarks (Cambridge Primary and Secondary Checkpoints), and rigorous focus on mathematics, sciences, and English fundamentals that give students ironclad academic confidence.
